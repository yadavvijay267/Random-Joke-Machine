# 😂 Random Joke Machine

> Tap a button, get a fresh joke. 120 clean, family-friendly jokes across 6 categories — with punchline reveals, ratings, favourites and a history of everything you've seen.

---

## ✨ About

**Random Joke Machine** is a small fun page for a quick laugh. Every tap brings a new joke, and jokes never repeat until you have seen them all. The punchline stays hidden until you reveal it, you can rate how funny each joke was, and the ones you love go into your favourites. Great for a mood lift — and a handy source of content ideas for social media.

## 🚀 Features

- 🎲 **120 jokes, 6 categories** — Dad Jokes, Puns, One-Liners, Animals, Tech and Food (20 each)
- 🔁 **No repeats** — a joke won't come back until you've seen the whole set
- 🙈 **Punchline reveal** — tap to unblur the punchline when you're ready
- ⭐ **Rate the joke** — mark how funny it was
- ❤️ **Favourites** — save jokes in a list and copy them all at once
- 🕘 **Just Seen history** — revisit jokes from earlier in the session
- 📋 **Copy any joke** — one tap to share it anywhere

## 🎮 How to Use

1. Tap **New Joke** (or press Space) for a fresh joke.
2. Guess the punchline, then tap to reveal it.
3. Rate it, copy it, or save it to favourites.
